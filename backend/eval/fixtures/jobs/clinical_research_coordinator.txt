Job Title: Clinical Research Coordinator
Company: Aurelia Health Institute
Location: On-site, Kuala Lumpur

Purpose of the role
Coordinate day-to-day operations of oncology clinical trials, from patient recruitment through to
regulatory submission, ensuring compliance with study protocols.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in nursing, pharmacy, life sciences or a related health discipline.
- 3+ years of experience coordinating clinical trials in a hospital or CRO setting.
- Working knowledge of Good Clinical Practice and ICH-GCP guidelines.
- Experience preparing submissions for institutional review boards and ethics committees.
- Accurate case report form completion and source data verification.
- Direct patient-facing communication and informed consent administration.

Nice to have
- Certification as a Clinical Research Coordinator (CCRC).
- Experience with electronic data capture systems such as Medidata Rave.
- Oncology therapeutic area experience.

Responsibilities
- Screen and enrol study participants against protocol eligibility criteria.
- Maintain trial master files and prepare for sponsor monitoring visits.
- Schedule study visits and coordinate sample collection with laboratory staff.
- Report adverse events within required timelines.
